Your Roborock,
in Apple Home.

A Homebridge plugin that puts your Roborock robot vacuums in the Home app, over HomeKit or Matter. Start a clean with Siri or an automation, and keep control on your home network even when the Roborock cloud is down.

Get started View on GitHub

Free and open source. Works with Homebridge 1.6 or later; Matter needs Homebridge 2.

Robot Vacuum
Rooms to clean

Try it. Pick rooms, or none for the whole home. Room cleaning needs Matter and iOS 18.4 or later.

Keeps working when the cloud doesn't

The Roborock cloud is only needed to sign in and to read your device list. Commands go straight to the vacuum over your home network, and if the cloud is unreachable when Homebridge starts, the plugin starts from its saved device list. Status, consumables and scenes are read and run locally too, with the cloud only as the fallback.

  1. Home appiPhone, iPad, Mac or HomePod
  2. HomeKit or Matter
  3. HomebridgeRuns the plugin
  4. Your home network
  5. Roborock vacuumOn your Wi-Fi

The plugin needs one successful online start to save the device list. A vacuum shared with you from another Roborock account can only be controlled through the cloud.

What you get in the Home app

Each vacuum shows up with the controls and readings it actually has.

  • Start, stop and send home

    Over HomeKit the vacuum is a fan-style tile: turn it on to start cleaning, off to stop and return to the dock. Siri, scenes and automations work too.

  • Battery, charging and errors

    Battery level, charging state and a low-battery warning. A vacuum that reports an error or goes offline is flagged, and an error that stays after you fixed its cause can be cleared from the plugin settings.

  • Your Roborock scenes

    Scenes you made in the Roborock app become switches, so a full routine runs with one tap or from an automation.

  • Dock mop wash

    On docks that wash the mop, a switch starts or stops a wash and turns on whenever the dock is washing, however it started.

  • Filter and brushes

    Over HomeKit, the remaining life of the filter, main brush and side brush, with a reminder when one is nearly worn out.

  • Current room to MQTT

    Optionally publishes the room being cleaned to your MQTT broker, so lights or other automations can follow the vacuum.

Matter, for real vacuum controls Beta

Choose Matter for a vacuum and Apple Home shows it as a robot vacuum instead of a fan: pick the rooms to clean, switch between vacuum, mop or both, and send it back to the dock. It works with Google Home, Alexa and SmartThings as well.

Apple Home's support for Matter vacuums is still young. Expect an uncertified-accessory warning while pairing and the odd hiccup after iOS updates. For the steadiest setup, stay on HomeKit.

Choose per vacuum

Load your devices in the plugin settings and pick one of three for each vacuum.

  • HomeKit (HAP)

    The default. The steady, familiar fan-style tile.

  • Matter (Beta)

    Native vacuum controls and room cleaning. Needs Homebridge 2 with Matter turned on; room cleaning needs iOS 18.4 or later.

  • Skip

    Keeps a vacuum out of Apple Home, for example one shared from another home.

Switching a vacuum to another protocol re-creates its accessory, so give it its room and automations again afterwards.

Only in Xiaomi Home?

The plugin sees the vacuums on your Roborock account. A vacuum that is set up only in the Xiaomi Home app is not on that account, so it won't show up.

Set it up in a few minutes

  1. Install the plugin

    In the Homebridge UI, open Plugins, search for Roborock Vacuum and install homebridge-roborock-vacuum.

  2. Sign in

    In the plugin settings, enter your Roborock app email and sign in with your password or with a code sent by email. The region is detected for you.

  3. Choose and restart

    Press Load devices, pick HomeKit, Matter or Skip for each vacuum, then restart Homebridge.

Requires Homebridge 1.6 or later and Node.js 18.20, 20.15, 22 or 24. Matter needs Homebridge 2 with Matter turned on. Full documentation
